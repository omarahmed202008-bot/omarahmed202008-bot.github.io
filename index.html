02 — Research practice

How an idea becomes
a testable claim.

Scientific maturity is not the absence of uncertainty; it is the discipline to define, measure, interpret, and report it responsibly.

01 / FORMULATE

Define the question

Start with a specific technical or social problem. Identify what a useful system should do—and how its success would be measured.

02 / TEST

Design the comparison

Build or reproduce a method, establish relevant baselines, and document assumptions, controls, and available evidence.

03 / INTERPRET

Report the limitation

Distinguish achieved observations from design goals, source-reported outcomes, projected impact, and unverified claims.

Adaptive World Models
PROJECT 01 / 06 — INDEPENDENT AI RESEARCH

Adaptive World Models

When environments change, can planning adapt without relying on false certainty?

2026AI & MODEL-BASED CONTROLUnpublished research manuscript
01 / Research question

What was investigated?

Can a sequential detector recognize changed dynamics, and does adapting planning depth improve control performance?

An experimental study of uncertainty signals, sequential change detection, and adaptive planning under abrupt environment shifts.

02 / Design & methods

From concept to investigation

Simplified conceptual sequence, based on the manuscript; not a reproduction of its exact implementation diagrams.

Five bootstrap-trained transition models, a CUSUM-style change detector, and comparisons among fixed, random, disagreement-based, evidence-guided and true-model planners across synthetic navigation settings.

  1. Train an ensemble of transition models on synthetic navigation interactions.
  2. Compare evidence-based change detection with disagreement and rolling surprise signals.
  3. Benchmark adaptive planning horizons against fixed and other baselines; report both computational work and reward.

Bar lengths reflect source-reported values; the original analysis reports no established reward gain.

In the held-out global-shift tests, the detector identified all eight changes, with median delay 17.5 observations. Evidence-guided planning reduced counted planning work by 41.1%, but achieved lower mean reward (0.078 per interaction) than fixed-horizon planning (0.112).

The system recognized changes and reduced planning work, but this did not establish a reward improvement over fixed-horizon planning.

04 / Ownership & attribution

Individual contribution

Author of the research manuscript; conducted the documented AI-assisted reproduction, experiment analysis, and reporting workflow.

Independent author; AI assistance disclosed in the manuscript.

05 / Limitations & next steps

What remains unresolved?

The manuscript explicitly reports a small synthetic benchmark, AI-assisted implementation and drafting, an unpublished draft, and no general reward advantage. Its negative result is part of the contribution.

Next evidence to add: Independent replication, larger environments, and broader benchmark suites are needed before generalizing.

Feedback-control abstraction derived from the supplied team report.
