Privacy, accessibility & research integrity

Privacy and analytics

This website has no third-party advertising, analytics scripts, cookies for tracking, or visitor-profiling code. The site's search and interactive research demos run locally in your browser; their input is not submitted to a server. GitHub Pages, the hosting service, may process request and diagnostic data in accordance with GitHub's own policies, which are separate from this site's script behavior.

The website author can review aggregate repository traffic through GitHub repository traffic insights. GitHub traffic statistics are limited and may not reflect all website visits. No individual visitor details are gathered by this portfolio's custom scripts. There is no third-party analytics provider connected.

Contact and public information

Contact options shown publicly are an email address and phone number provided for professional inquiries. No contact form stores messages. Clicking an email or telephone link opens your own email or phone application.

Accessibility

The site uses semantic headings, labeled navigation and form controls, keyboard-accessible interactive elements, visible focus indicators, and responsive layouts. Use Ctrl + K (or ⌘ + K on Mac) to open the portfolio search. Interactive research pages can be printed or saved to PDF using the browser, and each offers a plain-text project-summary download.

Scientific evidence

Experimental findings are distinguished from source-reported results, models, goals, educational examples and projects awaiting independent verification. No original project posters are embedded or offered for download. Educational sliders do not reproduce trained research models.

Copyright and collaborators

Project coauthors are acknowledged where the original documentation identifies them. Source materials and contributors' private documents are not offered as public downloads without appropriate permissions. Please use the research summaries for general academic review and credit collaborators accordingly.
